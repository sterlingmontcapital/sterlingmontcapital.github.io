STERLINGMONT CAPITAL / ABOUT

A clear perspective.
A longer horizon.

Australian private capital, shaped by purpose, alignment and a considered view of the future.

01 / WHO WE ARECAPITAL WITH PURPOSE

Connecting capital
with possibility.

Sterlingmont Capital is an Australian private capital firm focused on raising and deploying private capital with a long-term perspective.

Our model brings capital partners and investment opportunities together through considered structures and a disciplined assessment of risk, commercial fundamentals and alignment.

We believe the quality of a capital decision begins well before a transaction: with the right questions, clear objectives and an honest view of uncertainty.

OUR PERSPECTIVE

Capital is a commitment.
Perspective gives it direction.

Our philosophy places the purpose of capital alongside the commercial detail - connecting what an opportunity requires with what its stakeholders seek to achieve.

02 / GUIDING PRINCIPLESA CONSISTENT FOUNDATION
01

Considered capital

Start with the commercial purpose. Understand the capital requirement, the operating context and the assumptions supporting the opportunity.

Our starting question: what does this capital need to achieve?

02

Aligned interests

Clarity of expectations matters. Structures should address stakeholder objectives, responsibilities and how decisions will be made.

Our starting question: how will the relationship work in practice?

03

Long-term perspective

Look beyond the immediate transaction. Consider resilience, changing conditions and the pathways through which value could be realised.

Our starting question: what could change, and what would that mean?

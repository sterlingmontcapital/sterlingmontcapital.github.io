01 / BUSINESS GROWTH

Supporting the next chapter.

Private capital can help a business navigate expansion, ownership change or a new stage of commercial development. A clear use of funds and a realistic operating plan are central to understanding the opportunity.

Questions for assessment

  • Commercial purpose and use of capital
  • Business model, cash flows and operating assumptions
  • Governance and stakeholder alignment
02

02 / ASSET-BACKED OPPORTUNITIES

Look through to the fundamentals.

Tangible assets can provide a basis for analysis, but asset backing alone does not remove investment risk. Valuation, cash generation, structure and practical realisation pathways all require consideration.

Questions for assessment

  • Asset quality and independent valuation
  • Cash generation and relevant obligations
  • Structure, enforceability and realisation risks
03

03 / SPECIAL SITUATIONS

Clarity within complexity.

Transitional or complex circumstances may call for a tailored capital structure and a patient assessment of uncertainty. The focus is on understanding the drivers of change and testing the available pathways.

Questions for assessment

  • Nature of the transition and decision timeline
  • Execution dependencies and downside scenarios
  • Information quality and potential exit pathways
PERSPECTIVE ON RISK

Private investments can involve loss of capital, limited liquidity, valuation uncertainty and execution risk. Illustrative focus areas do not imply that a particular investment is available or suitable.

STERLINGMONT CAPITAL / INVESTMENT APPROACH

Conviction begins
with questions.

A deliberate framework for understanding opportunity, testing assumptions and maintaining perspective.

01 / THE FRAMEWORKDISCIPLINE AT EVERY STAGE

Purpose first.
Detail throughout.

An investment thesis should be understandable, challengeable and grounded in the commercial fundamentals.

The framework below expresses Sterlingmont's intended approach to private capital. Each opportunity would require its own assessment, documentation and decisions.

01

STAGE 01

Understand the opportunity

Start with the purpose.

Define the capital requirement and the commercial objectives. Understand the business or asset, the operating environment and the expectations of the parties involved.

  • Commercial purpose
  • Stakeholder objectives
  • Information requirements
02

STAGE 02

Assess the fundamentals

Test the assumptions.

Examine the information supporting the investment thesis. Consider cash flows, asset quality, governance, legal structure and the effect of alternative scenarios.

  • Due diligence
  • Scenario analysis
  • Independent input where appropriate
03

STAGE 03

Structure for alignment

Make expectations explicit.

Consider suitable terms, responsibilities and decision rights. Address the relationship between the proposed capital structure, identified risks and stakeholder objectives.

  • Terms and responsibilities
  • Governance and reporting
  • Conditions before commitment
04

STAGE 04

Maintain active oversight

Keep perspective as conditions change.

Establish a framework for monitoring, reporting and review. Revisit the original assumptions and consider how developments may affect the objectives and available pathways.

  • Reporting framework
  • Ongoing review
  • Realisation considerations

AN INDEPENDENT VIEW OF UNCERTAINTY

Risk belongs in
the conversation.

A disciplined process does not eliminate risk or guarantee an outcome.

Private investments can be illiquid and may lose value. Any assessment needs to consider the specific opportunity, available information and the ability to absorb adverse outcomes.

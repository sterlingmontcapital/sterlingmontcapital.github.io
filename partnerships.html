BEFORE A FIRST CONVERSATION

Start with
the essentials.

A concise introduction is often the most useful place to begin.

What should an introduction include?+

A high-level description of the business or relationship, the purpose of the enquiry and an indication of the intended timing.

Should I send confidential documents?+

Please avoid sending sensitive information in an initial introduction. Any appropriate confidentiality arrangements and document channels would need to be agreed separately.

Is an introduction an investment application?+

No. Corporate dialogue does not constitute an investment application, an offer of capital or a commitment by any party.

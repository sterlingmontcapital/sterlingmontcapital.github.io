01 / ABOUT STERLINGMONTCAPITAL WITH PURPOSE

A clear perspective.
A longer horizon.

Sterlingmont Capital is an Australian private capital firm focused on raising and deploying private capital with a long-term perspective.

Our model brings capital partners and investment opportunities together through considered structures and a disciplined assessment of risk, commercial fundamentals and alignment.

We believe enduring value starts with understanding what capital needs to achieve - and the people, assets and businesses behind it.

03 / INVESTMENT APPROACHDISCIPLINE AT EVERY STAGE

Conviction begins
with questions.

A deliberate process for understanding opportunity, testing assumptions and maintaining perspective.

Clarity of purpose.
Strength of process.

Our approach in detail ↗
01 Understand the opportunity +

Establish the commercial purpose, capital requirements and stakeholder objectives. Start with the underlying business case.

02 Assess the fundamentals +

Examine cash flows, asset quality, governance and relevant risks. Test assumptions against alternative scenarios and independent information.

03 Structure for alignment +

Consider appropriate terms, responsibilities and decision rights. Define expectations clearly before capital is committed.

04 Maintain active oversight +

Set a framework for reporting, monitoring and review, with attention to changing conditions and the objectives established at the outset.
